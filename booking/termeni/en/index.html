How the booking is made

  • you choose the arrival and departure dates and the number of guests;
  • you see the available rooms and the total price for the stay, before anything else;
  • you enter your name, phone number, email, and city;
  • you press "Send booking".

From that moment the booking is recorded with us and you receive the confirmation number by email, together with a link from which you can review or cancel it. We also contact you by phone to confirm. In some cases the room is only held for you until you confirm from the email you received; if you don't confirm within the time stated there, it releases itself and nothing is charged.

The contract is concluded the moment we confirm your booking. If the room was taken between your search and your submission, we'll tell you right away and show you what's available again.

Prices

Prices are in lei and include VAT. They are shown per room and per night, and you see the total for the stay before submitting the booking. The price in the confirmation is the price you pay; no extra fees or commissions appear on arrival.

Payment

Payment is made on arrival, at the front desk, in cash or by card. The booking does not require online payment and does not retain any card details.

When the online payment option is enabled, you can pay by card through NETOPIA Payments. You enter the card details exclusively on the processor's secure page, with 3-D Secure; they never pass through us and are not stored on our servers. The amount is in lei, the same as in the confirmation.

The invoice is issued by S.C. OVISER S.R.L., under the name and details you give us, and you receive it by email or at the front desk.

Changes and cancellation

You can cancel from the link in the confirmation email — the button opens a page where you confirm, so nothing is cancelled by mistake — or through the withdrawal form. For a change (different dates, a different room), write or call us and we'll rework it together.

Cancelling a booking charges the full value of the first night's stay. The remaining amount, if paid in advance, is refunded to the same card within 14 days of the cancellation. A no-show on the arrival date is treated as a cancellation. Details are in the cancellation policy.

Right of withdrawal

Under Article 16, letter l of Government Emergency Ordinance 34/2014, accommodation contracts concluded for a specific date or period are exempt from the 14-day right of withdrawal. Even so, we offer the cancellation described above, and you can send a withdrawal request online, at any time, through the withdrawal form — the channel required by Article 11¹ of the same ordinance.
