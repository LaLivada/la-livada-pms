Withdrawal from contract

The withdrawal request.

Fill in your name, email, and booking number, press "Send" and you'll get a copy right away. We'll confirm the cancellation by email.

This form is the online way to exercise your right of withdrawal, under Article 11¹ of Government Emergency Ordinance 34/2014. Accommodation booked for a specific date is exempt by law from the 14-day withdrawal period (Article 16, letter l), but we offer you the cancellation described in the cancellation policy anyway: cancelling charges the full first night's stay, and the rest is refunded.

Other ways

  • from the confirmation email, the "Cancel booking" button — the fastest way, the cancellation happens instantly;
  • by email, at office@lalivada.com, with your booking number;
  • by phone, at +40 722 899 899.

What we do with the request

We record it in our booking system and send it by email to the front desk, and a copy to you. We respond within 14 days at most, usually the same day. We use the data from the form only to resolve the request; how we keep it is described on the Privacy page.
